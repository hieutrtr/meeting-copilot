### Misclassified samples (the 2 FPs)

Both are "embedded question inside a declarative" — exactly the pattern Stage 2 (T-2.3 Haiku filter) is designed to catch:

| ID | Text | Why it tripped |
|---|---|---|
| `n-en-11` | "I'm not sure what is the right answer here." | `WH_EN_AUX` matched `what is`. Declarative-leader filter only suppresses bare wh, not wh-aux pairs. |
| `n-en-12` | "Let me figure out how does this work." | `WH_EN_AUX` matched `how does`. Same shape as above. |

Both were placed in the fixture intentionally (`tag: "embedded-question-trap"`) to verify the precision floor is realistic and not a result of cherry-picked easy negatives. Tightening the rule (e.g. requiring sentence-initial wh) would risk recall on legitimate utterances like "So how does it work?" — explicitly the tradeoff Stage 2 absorbs.
